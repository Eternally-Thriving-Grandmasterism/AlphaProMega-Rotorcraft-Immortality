## 3. What Is Frozen

- Residual limit load / torque / yaw authority magnitude definitions for Rank 1–4 residual (primary-failed / isolated / damaged / post-healing) configurations, expressed as equal to the corresponding design residual capability demands
- Ultimate / residual design magnitude definitions as residual limit × residual factor of safety (WP-07), with factors remaining parametric
- Transition magnitude character bounded by residual limit sets, with transient amplification deferred
- Rules requiring residual configurations for residual capability substantiation and full traceability to frozen constructions and requirements
- Explicit parametric status until vehicle class, maximum weight, residual demand, and certification basis residual factors are frozen
