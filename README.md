## The Problem Set (Documented Reality)

From accident databases, NTSB, AAIB, military reports, and industry surveys:

| Failure Class | Typical Consequence | Why It Happens |
|---------------|---------------------|----------------|
| Main rotor retention ("Jesus nut" / mast nut) | Rotor departure → freefall | Single critical fastener |
| Main gearbox / transmission | Loss of drive, seizure, fire | High torque, fatigue, loss of lubrication, single load path |
| Tail rotor / anti-torque system | Uncontrollable yaw | Single mechanical path, gearbox, or drive shaft |
| Engine power loss (esp. single-engine) | Autorotation required (skill + altitude dependent) | Fuel, mechanical, ingestion |
| Hydraulic system | Loss of control authority | Leaks, contamination, single systems |
| Rotor blade fatigue / delamination | Blade separation or loss of lift | High-cycle fatigue, manufacturing, erosion |
| Corrosion (esp. maritime / salt) | Unexpected structural or mechanical failure | Environment + material |
| Maintenance-induced ("infant mortality") | Failure shortly after work | Improper assembly, torque, incomplete procedures |
| Vibration / imbalance / bearings | Cascade into structural or gearbox failure | Progressive |

Fatigue accounts for ~55% of component failures in long-lived fleets. Gearboxes remain a classic single-point-of-failure domain because mechanical gear trains are difficult to fully redundant without weight and complexity penalties.

## Architectural Pillars (TOLC-Aligned Solutions)

### 1. Zero-Single-Point-of-Failure Rotor Retention
- Eliminate classic Jesus-nut geometry.
- Multi-path, fail-safe retention systems with independent load paths and continuous integrity monitoring.
- Redundant locking and secondary retention that can carry full flight loads if primary fails.

### 2. Distributed / Redundant Drive Architecture
- Move beyond single main gearbox + single tail-rotor gearbox.
- Explore and mature:
  - Split-torque / multi-path mechanical gearboxes with isolation.
  - Hybrid electric / electromagnetic drive concepts that reduce or eliminate high-speed gear meshes.
  - Dual or multi independent power paths to main rotor and anti-torque system.
- Continuous health: chip detection + vibration + temperature + oil-condition fused into predictive models.

### 3. Daedalus-Skin Self-Healing Structures
- Self-healing composite and hybrid lattices for blades, spars, fuselage, and critical attachments.
- Microvascular or microcapsule healing agents + embedded sensing network.
- Ability to recover from fatigue cracks, impact, and limited ballistic/debris damage in flight or on ground.

### 4. Predictive AGI Health Lattice (Ra-Thor / PATSAGi integration)
- Onboard and cloud-hybrid continuous monitoring under valence/mercy gates.
- Hierarchical predictive coding of vibration, acoustic, thermal, oil chemistry, and structural strain.
- Early warning that forces landing or reconfiguration long before catastrophic transition.
- Maintenance error detection (post-maintenance signature anomalies).

### 5. Multi-Engine / Hybrid Propulsion with Graceful Degradation
- Twin or multi-engine baselines with true single-engine capability for the mission profile.
- Hybrid-electric assist for autorotation energy management and emergency power.
- Fuel system isolation and contamination resilience.

### 6. Corrosion & Environmental Fortress
- Advanced coatings, sealed systems, and material selection for maritime and all-weather operations.
- Self-healing surface layers where practical.

### 7. Human + Machine Co-Pilot Safety
- Mercy-gated flight control and envelope protection that still respects pilot authority.
- Automated emergency procedures that buy time and options rather than replace the human.
